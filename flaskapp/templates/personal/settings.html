Cycles

Every exercise runs its own four week cycle, tracked automatically from your logs.
  • An exercise moves to the next week once you complete its heaviest set for the target reps.
  • Miss the week, or miss the load, and it starts again at week 1 from the next Monday.
  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday, and exercises are never synchronised with each other.
  • After a full four week cycle the dashboard offers a target weight increase.
